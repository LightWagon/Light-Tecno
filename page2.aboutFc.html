About This Website

Technology, made easier to Explore

This website was developed as an academic project at Father Saturnino Urios University (FSUU). Its purpose is to provide a convenient platform where users can explore smartphones and electronic devices, view their specifications, and compare prices to make more informed decisions.

The website's design draws inspiration from Dior's elegant and minimalist presentation and Samsung's modern, technology-focused aesthetic. These two brands helped guide the website's visual direction and organization, combining Dior's sophisticated style with Samsung's innovative approach to technology. The website's layout and color palette were then customized to create a cohesive design suited to this project.

About me

I am a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at Father Saturnino Urios University (FSUU). My interest in technology began during my high school years at Butuan City Comprehensive High School, where I took the Computer Systems Servicing (CSS) strand under the Technical-Vocational-Livelihood (TVL) track.

During my studies, I developed a broader understanding of computer hardware and gained introductory experience in SQL programming by creating a calculator. I also gained practical experience in network setup, including configuring a small server, during my on-the-job training (OJT) at Couples For Christ School of the Morning Star.

Currently, at FSUU, I am expanding my knowledge of programming and exploring how computers work at a deeper level, particularly how code is written, processed, and executed by a computer. These experiences continue to strengthen my interest in information technology and motivate me to develop my technical skills further.
